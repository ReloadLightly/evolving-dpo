Smaller, but still usable -- this subset is a genuine test rather than

## 2. H1, seed-level -- the criterion the preregistration actually states

"Non-overlapping 95% CIs across 5 seeds" is a test on 5 numbers per arm.
Dataset size does not enter; between-seed SD does.

| Between-seed SD | Non-overlapping 95% CI needs | Two-sample t needs | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 pp | **1.24 pp** | 1.01 pp | 1.23x |
| 1.0 pp | **2.48 pp** | 2.02 pp | 1.23x |
| 2.0 pp | **4.97 pp** | 4.04 pp | 1.23x |
| 3.0 pp | **7.45 pp** | 6.06 pp | 1.23x |

Two consequences worth deciding on deliberately:

1. **Non-overlapping CIs is stricter than p < 0.05.** Two means whose 95%
   CIs merely touch are separated at about p = 0.006. The criterion is
   defensible and conservative, but it is not "significance", and it
   should be described as the stronger claim it is.
2. **With 5 seeds, between-seed SD dominates everything.** If seeds vary by
   2 pp, no amount of test data lets the criterion resolve a 3 pp effect.
   Measure the seed SD in the first arm you train; if it is above ~1 pp,
   raise the seed count before running all five arms.

### If the topic split uses every year instead of 2024 alone

The temporal and topic splits are separate designs (HANDOFF section 5).
H3 holds out *areas*, not years, so it may draw on the whole corpus --
as long as the paper says plainly that H3 is not additionally held out
in time, and the temporal result is reported separately.

- All pairs 2020-2024: **9,999**, balanced: **6,096**
- Per test area (2 areas): **3,048**
- Detectable interaction: **4.97 pp** (vs 8.25 pp on 2024 alone)

## What this means for the design

- **Use few, large areas.** Interaction power scales with the *smallest*
  cell, so splitting into many fine-grained areas is the fastest way to
  make H3 untestable. Two well-separated areas beat six precise ones.
- **SNOR has no `primary_area`.** The split must be built from `keywords`
  or by clustering the Specter embeddings SNOR ships. Build it to
  maximize cell size subject to the two areas being genuinely distinct,
  and fix the choice before training (HANDOFF section 5).
- **Add ICLR 2025.** It roughly doubles the test set, and the interaction
  is the analysis that needs it most.
- **Report the interaction with its CI even if null.** A null with a tight
  CI says DPO does not capture a documented difference; a null with a wide
  CI says nothing at all. Only the power analysis distinguishes them, and
  that distinction is most of H3's value either way.
